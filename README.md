## About

Second-year B.Tech student in AI & Data Science at VESIT, Mumbai (Batch 2025–2029). Most of what I know has come from building — hackathon projects, small apps, experiments, and plenty of trial and error.

Currently exploring **backend development and systems**, while continuing to sharpen my problem-solving through DSA. I enjoy figuring out how things work, turning ideas into projects, and learning by building. I also use AI-assisted tools to move faster, but I care about understanding what I build rather than blindly shipping generated code.
